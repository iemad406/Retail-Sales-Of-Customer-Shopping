📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior and sales performance across multiple shopping malls in Istanbul. The goal is to uncover insights related to pricing patterns, customer demographics, and revenue distribution using data analysis techniques.

🗂️ Dataset Description

The dataset contains transactional sales data from 10 shopping malls in Istanbul, including:

Invoice Number
Invoice Date
Customer ID
Product Category
Price
Shopping Mall
Customer Age
Gender
Payment Method

Dataset Size: 99,457 invoices
Data Quality:

No missing values
No duplicate records
Clean and reliable for analysis
🎯 Objectives
Analyze price distribution across categories and malls
Identify high-revenue categories and malls
Study customer spending behavior by age and gender
Evaluate payment method preferences
Provide business insights and recommendations
🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
📌 Price Distribution Analysis
Scatter plots were used to analyze price distribution across categories and malls
Prices are generally consistent across different malls
Significant price differences exist between categories

Key Insight:

Mall of Istanbul and Kanyon show strong revenue performance but pricing remains category-driven rather than location-based
📌 Category Analysis
Technology category has the highest prices (reaching over 5000)
Lower-priced categories:
Toys
Books
Clothing
Shoes

Insight:

Clear price gap between premium and low-cost categories
Brand positioning likely affects pricing (especially in clothing & shoes)
📌 Statistical Analysis
Used Mean, Variance, and Standard Deviation to compare pricing across malls

Example:

Cevahir AVM vs Emaar Square Mall (Books category)

Findings:

Higher variance in Emaar Square Mall
Indicates slightly higher price dispersion compared to Cevahir

📈 Key Insights Summary
Pricing is category-driven, not mall-dependent
Technology products dominate high-value sales
Older customers (40+) are the primary revenue drivers
Female customers contribute more to overall spending
Cash remains the dominant payment method
Revenue is concentrated in a few top malls
